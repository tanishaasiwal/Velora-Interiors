A LITTLE CLARITY

Good design starts with good questions.

Here are a few things clients often ask us before getting started.

What types of spaces does Vélora design?

We design homes, villas, apartments, offices, cafés, restaurants, and hospitality spaces. Our services can cover a complete interior or focus on the areas that matter most to you.

How does the design process begin?

We start with a conversation about your space, lifestyle, priorities, and inspiration. From there, we shape a design direction and project scope around your needs.

How long does an interior project take?

Timelines depend on the size of the space, design scope, and execution requirements. After learning more about your project, we can outline a considered schedule for each stage.

Can you work within my budget?

Yes. We discuss your budget early and use it to guide material, furniture, and scope decisions. A tailored estimate follows once we understand the space and your priorities.

Where do you take on projects?

We take on projects across India. Depending on the location and scope, design discussions can begin remotely, with site visits planned as the project requires.
